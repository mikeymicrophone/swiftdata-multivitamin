# Delete rules · 09 · Qamsar correction line

The same day the clear slip is corrected to "bright", and the long rose to "shorter rose".

**What it teaches**

- The cook cascades to its slips, and each slip cascades to its correction.
- Voiding the cook removes the slips and the corrections.
