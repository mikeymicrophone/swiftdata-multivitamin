# Transient · 10 · Twin lumps

On 11 April Kemal Aydın cuts spare lump EB-8, 210 grams, while EB-7 is already at the bedesten.

**What it teaches**

- The open EB-7 card says avoid the grey seam. The open EB-8 card says clear at the foot.
- A new context empties both notes and keeps both lump codes and both weights.
